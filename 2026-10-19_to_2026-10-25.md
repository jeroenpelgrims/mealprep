Daily budget: ~1,100–1,150 kcal across lunch + dinner.
**Revised twice: (1) protein reduced — lunch + dinner now ~57–61 g/day (~87–101 g/day incl. 30–40 g breakfast); (2) broccoli swapped for carrots (FODMAP-free, easier to digest). Same recipes, same prep flow — smaller meat portions, bigger carb portions.**

## 4. Prep instructions (~90 min, one session)

1. **Instant Pot — rice:** 400 g rice + ~480 ml water, high pressure 4 min, natural release. Fluff and set aside.
2. **Oven batch 1 (200°C, ~25 min):** meatballs (500 g turkey + 1 tsp paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + salt & pepper, rolled into ~15 balls) + 2 kg sweet potato wedges (olive oil, salt, paprika) spread over two trays, with 750 g carrots cut into sticks (olive oil, salt — the lunch share) alongside. Swap trays halfway.
3. **Instant Pot — filling:** sauté the remaining 500 g turkey with 1 tsp paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + salt; stir in ~150 g salsa and all the cooked rice.
4. **Oven batch 2 (200°C, ~25 min):** 10 bell pepper halves, cut side down, 10 min; flip, fill with turkey-rice mix, top with 20 g cheese each, bake ~15 min more.
5. **Oven batch 3 (200°C, ~25 min):** 1.8 kg potato wedges (olive oil, salt) + 600 g carrot sticks (the dinner share) first 10 min; add the pork tenderloin (coated in 2 tbsp Dijon + 1 tbsp dried herbs). Rest pork 5 min, then slice into 4 portions.

## 6. Anything else

- Shopping quantities include a ~5–10% margin over raw portion weights to cover cooking losses.
- Turkey dropped from 1.8 kg → ~1.05 kg and pork from 750 g → ~400 g; sweet potatoes, potatoes and rice grew to keep calories steady. Same 10 items, same session structure.
- With turkey meals spanning 5+ days: refrigerate the first 3 days' portions and freeze the rest right after prep; thaw overnight in the fridge.
- Pork tenderloin dries out if overcooked — pull it at ~63–65°C internal if you have a thermometer.
- Broccoli (high FODMAP) was swapped for carrots, which are effectively FODMAP-free at any portion size. If digestion still doesn't improve, the next suspect is the salsa — most jars contain onion and garlic; swap it for plain passata + a pinch of cumin.
