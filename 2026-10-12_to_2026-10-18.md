# Weekly Food Prep Plan — Lunch & Dinner (~1,200 kcal/day)

## 1. Meals

**Lunches**
- Chicken rice bowl — 4 days
- Turkey chili with rice — 3 days

**Dinners**
- Salmon, potato & green bean tray bake — 4 days
- Turkey chili with rice — 3 days

The chili is one big batch of 6 portions, split across lunches and dinners so you never eat it twice on the same day (e.g. chili lunch Mon/Wed/Fri, chili dinner Tue/Thu/Sat).

## 2. Ingredients (shopping list)

| # | Amount | Ingredient |
|---|--------|------------|
| 1 | 800 g | Chicken breast |
| 2 | 1 kg | Turkey mince (5–7% fat) |
| 3 | 4 × 150 g | Salmon fillets |
| 4 | 500 g | White rice (dry) |
| 5 | 1 kg | Potatoes |
| 6 | 600 g | Broccoli (frozen) |
| 7 | 600 g | Green beans (frozen) |
| 8 | 3 × 400 g cans | Kidney beans |
| 9 | 2 × 400 g cans | Chopped tomatoes |
| 10 | 1 large | Onion |
| 11 | 1 packet | Chili seasoning |

Pantry (assumed on hand): olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder.

## 3. Prep session (~1 hour, everything cooks in parallel)

1. **Rice (Instant Pot):** 440 g dry rice + 440 g water, 4 min high pressure, 10 min natural release. Fluff and set aside.
2. **Chili (Instant Pot, same pot):** add turkey mince, drained kidney beans, chopped tomatoes, chopped onion, chili seasoning. Stir, 10 min high pressure, quick release.
3. **Chicken + broccoli (air fryer):** season chicken with salt, pepper, paprika; 200 °C for 18–20 min, flip once. Then broccoli with a little oil, 200 °C for 6–8 min.
4. **Salmon tray (oven):** cubed potatoes + oil on a tray, 200 °C for 15 min; add salmon and green beans, 12 min more.

**Daily total: ~1,170–1,185 kcal** depending on the combination — right at the 1,200 kcal budget, with ~75–80 g protein per day from these two meals.

## 5. Anything else

- **Freezing:** cooked food keeps 3–4 days in the fridge. If you prep on Sunday, freeze the portions for Friday onward (2 bowls, 2 chili, 2 salmon) and move them to the fridge the night before.
- **Chili is dump-and-go:** no need to brown the turkey first — pressure cooking handles it. Stir well after cooking to break up the mince.
- Calories are approximate; salmon is the most variable (wild fillets run ~30% lower than farmed).
